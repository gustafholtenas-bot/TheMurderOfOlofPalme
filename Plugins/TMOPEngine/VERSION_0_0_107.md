# TMOP Engine 0.0.107

Newspaper reader presentation correction:

- The default reader no longer attaches the newspaper to `hand_rSocket`.
- The newspaper floats in front of the dedicated reading camera at an
  editor-adjustable waist-height offset with a small default tilt.
- Optional first-person arms remain available but are disabled by default.
- The folded mesh is used for the front cover and, rotated 180 degrees, for
  the back cover. The open mesh is used for inner two-page spreads.
- Next/previous navigation advances by physical spreads and direct page jumps
  are normalized to the left page of a spread.
